# SUPERHOT Takeover

**Portal 2** × SUPERHOT · visual · idea by the team

Bring SUPERHOT into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I dropped SUPERHOT into Portal 2: time only moves when I move, and the red glass turrets shoot slow bullets right through my portals.

## Install

Copy the mod folder's scripts, sound and materials into a new "sigf" folder next to Portal 2's portal2 folder, add a gameinfo.txt that mounts portal2, then launch Portal 2 with -game sigf +sv_cheats 1 +map sp_a2_turret_intro. Stand still for slow motion, move to speed time up, and ram the red turrets.
